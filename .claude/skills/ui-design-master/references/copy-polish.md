# Copy aur Polish

Capability: `copy-polish`. Dimag ke STEP 5.8 se.

## Likhawat (copy)

Likhawat (copy): label chhota aur kriya wala ("Paste karo", "Bhejo", "Wapas"), "Submit" jaisa gol-matol nahi. Poori app mein ek jaisi awaaz: dostana par seedhi, bina jargon. Error mein user ka dosh nahi, sudhaar batao. Ank ke saath unit likho. Ek cheez ka ek hi naam poori app mein.

## Mana wale shabdon ki soochi (copy ledger)

Mana wale shabdon ki soochi (copy ledger): "Oops", "Something went wrong", "Kuch galat ho gaya", "Error 500", "Invalid input", "Aapne galti ki", "Submit", "Click here", "Please wait", "Seamless", "Revolutionary", "Supercharge", "Unlock". Har ek ki jagah wahi likho jo user ko kaam aaye: kya hua aur ab kya karein ("Link Instagram ka nahi lag raha. Dobara paste karein?"), kriya wala label ("Bhejo", "Paste karo"), aur seedhi bhasha. Output bhejne se pehle poore text mein in shabdon ko dhundh kar hatao.

## Polish ki list

Polish ki list (yaad dilane ke liye, isse bahar bhi socho aur sirf wahi jodo jo is product ko sach mein behtar dikhaye): skeleton ki halki chamak (shimmer); optimistic UI (turant dikhe, peeche confirm ho); pehchan tag ka slide-in (200ms); safal pal par halka checkmark animation aur Success haptic; ek zaroori ank par count-up (400ms, sirf ek baar); list se detail tak ek hi cheez ka smooth badlaav (shared element); list par pull-to-refresh; swipe action par haptic; mukhya button safe area ke upar tiki; pehli baar ke liye ek baar dikhne wala chhota guide; offline banner (shaant, daraoona nahi); keyboard focus ring; app icon, splash aur pehli screen ek jaise; dark mode ka barabar nikhaar.

## Hindi aur mishrit bhasha ka layout

Hindi aur mishrit bhasha: Devanagari text ko English se zyada line-height chahiye (lagbhag 1.5 se 1.6 guna) aur wo unchai mein katna nahi chahiye. Hindi, Hinglish aur English teeno mein button aur label ka layout na toote.
